# One-Pager: <Opportunity Name>

*Precedes a PRD — for when the opportunity is real but the solution isn't clear yet.*

## Opportunity
What problem or opportunity are we looking at, and why does it matter?

- Ground this in evidence you already have — a metric, user feedback, a market signal — not just an intuition that something's worth exploring.
- Say why it matters now specifically — what's the cost of waiting or ignoring it?

## Hypotheses
What do we believe is true about users/the market that makes this worth pursuing? What would prove or disprove it?

- Write each hypothesis so it's falsifiable — "users want faster checkout" isn't testable; "users abandon checkout when it takes more than 3 steps" is.
- Separate what you actually know from what you're assuming — the assumptions are exactly what the next section should be trying to validate.

## Possible approaches
A few directions worth considering, without committing to one yet.

- List genuinely different approaches, not variations on the same idea — the point is to show the option space before narrowing it.
- Note the rough tradeoff of each (cost, risk, time) even at this early stage — enough for an informed conversation, not a full spec.

## What we'd need to learn
Open questions to resolve before this becomes a PRD — link to the spike/research that should answer them.

- Prioritize the questions that could kill the opportunity entirely — answer those before the ones that only refine an approach.
- *If every question here can only be answered by building the real thing, a one-pager may not be the right vehicle — consider a spike instead.*

## Recommendation
Pursue further, park, or drop — and what the next concrete step is.

- Make the recommendation a decision, not a summary — "pursue" needs a next owner and next step, not just a leaning.
- If the recommendation is "park" or "drop," say what would change your mind later so this doesn't need to be re-litigated from scratch.
